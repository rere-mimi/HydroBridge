    Analysis length — from the drawn centreline

    Draw the centreline to set transect count.

    DEM area of interest The bridge pin is the centre. Upstream, downstream, and lateral distances define four edge points. Those points form a rectangle. HydroBridge crops that window from the LINZ 1 m LiDAR tiles instead of downloading whole Topo50 sheets.

    Distances from the pin. The rectangle rotates to the centreline (downstream is forward) or sits north-up until a line is drawn.

    Return period (ARI) Annual Recurrence Interval. Select one or more design floods and enter the flow for each. Water level and velocity for every selected ARI overlay on the same profile, with the centreline slope shown on the long section.

    Select one or more intervals. Enter Q for each selected ARI. Profiles overlay on the same plot.

    Elevation data

    New Zealand LiDAR 1 m DEM (LINZ 121859). HydroBridge crops the area of interest from the Topo50 tiles the rectangle touches. Whole sheets are not downloaded.

    When you pin a bridge, HydroBridge builds a rectangle from the upstream, downstream, and lateral extents about the pin, looks up the LINZ 1 m LiDAR sheets that intersect that polygon, and windows those COGs to the rectangle. Screening uses the cropped GeoTIFF.
