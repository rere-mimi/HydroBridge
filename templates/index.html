HydroBridge

Hydraulic screening at a bridge site

    Bridge location

    Click the map, search, or type coordinates. The pin is the site that will be screened, and the DEM in use is drawn over the map at 50% opacity.

    River centreline

    Elevation data

    HydroBridge clips the New Zealand LiDAR 1m DEM around the bridge pin and shows it on the map at 50% opacity. That nationwide layer is the default for every site.

    Transects and sampling

    Set the cross-section length, how far and how often they sit along the river, then the DEM sample spacing on each line.

    Flow

    Enter the design flow and Manning’s n. Slope is taken from the river centreline on the DEM. Water level is raised on each transect until Manning’s equation matches that flow.
